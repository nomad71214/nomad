## 信函的三种版式

三种版式的差别只在位置, 措辞一个字都不用改
由旧到新依次是缩进式, 齐头式, 混合式

### 缩进式

发件人地址, 日期, 结尾敬语, 署名靠右或居中
收件人地址与称呼靠左, 正文每段首行缩进
缩进量通常是五个空格
这是最传统的版式, 讲义说它略显过时

The first line of every paragraph is indented
(usually five spaces).

### 齐头式

所有内容顶到左边界, 四周留一英寸边距
发件人地址, 日期, 收件人地址, 称呼, 正文, 敬语, 署名
全部左对齐, 段与段之间空一行, 段首不缩进
这是今天商务场合最常用的版式

Everything is aligned to the left margin, including the
return address, the date, the inside address, salutation,
paragraphs, closing, and signature.

### 混合式

混合式是前两种的折中, 只有四样东西靠右或居中
发件人地址, 日期, 结尾敬语, 署名
其余全部左对齐: 收件人地址, 称呼, 正文
正文顶格, 段间空一行, 这一点与齐头式相同

The return address, date, closing, and signature are aligned
to the right or center, like in the indented format. But all
other parts, including paragraphs, are aligned to the left,
like in the block format.

从上到下的排布是

(1) 发件人地址, 靠右或居中
(2) 空一行写日期, 与地址左边缘对齐
(3) 空一行写收件人地址, 回到左边界
(4) 空一行写称呼, 后跟冒号
(5) 正文顶格, 段与段之间空一行
(6) 空一行写结尾敬语, 靠右或居中
(7) 留三行签名, 再打姓名与职务
(8) 附件另起一行, 与署名同一边

记住哪四样靠右, 混合式就不会写错
地址, 日期, 敬语, 署名, 少一样就退回了齐头式

表8-1 三种版式的差别

| 项目 | 缩进式 | 齐头式 | 混合式 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 地址与日期 | 靠右或居中 | 左对齐 | 靠右或居中 |
| 收件人与称呼 | 左对齐 | 左对齐 | 左对齐 |
| 段首 | 缩进五格 | 不缩进 | 不缩进 |
| 段间 | 空一行 | 空一行 | 空一行 |
| 敬语与署名 | 靠右或居中 | 左对齐 | 靠右或居中 |
| 使用场合 | 传统, 略过时 | 现代通用 | 雅致, 常用 |

改版式只动空白, 不动措辞
四段结构在三种版式里完全一样

## 信函的组成部分

八个部分, 英文名按出现顺序是
return address, date, inside address, salutation
body text, complimentary close, signature, enclosure

表8-2 信函的八个组成部分

| 部分 | 内容 | 要求 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 发件人地址 | 门牌, 城市, 州, 邮编 | 有信头纸时省略 |
| 日期 | 15 March 2005 | 全篇一种写法 |
| 收件人地址 | 姓名, 职务, 机构, 地址 | 与信封一致 |
| 称呼 | Dear Ms. Jones: | 后跟冒号 |
| 正文 | 四段, 每段一个任务 | 段间空一行 |
| 结尾敬语 | Sincerely, | 后跟逗号 |
| 署名 | 姓名与职务 | 与敬语对齐 |
| 附件 | Enclosed: 加内容 | 信末单列一行 |

日期有两种写法, 15 March 2005 与 March 15, 2005
一篇里只取一种, 不混着写

正式信件尽量不缩写, 月份与州名写全称
称呼后跟冒号, 也有用逗号的, 一封里只用一种
敬语后跟逗号, 空三行, 再打姓名与职务
附件在信末单列一行: Enclosed: Copy of the receipt for iron

## 信函的分类

按正式程度分两类
商务信函写给公司, 经理或不认识的人
私人信函写给亲友, 是非正式的一类

按目的与内容分
申请信, 感谢信, 询问信, 跟进信, 投诉信等
讲义列的原名是 application letters, thank-you letters,
inquiry letters, follow-up letters, complaint letters

投诉信属于正式的商务信函, 后面几节只谈这一类

## 投诉信的定义与要求

A complaint letter is a formal document that expresses your
dissatisfaction regarding a specific service or product that
fails to meet the claims made by the provider. Sometimes it
is an incident which causes you discomfort, as a result, you
request appropriate changes to be made and actions taken.

定义里有三件事
(1) 正式的文书, 不是随手写的抱怨
(2) 不满指向具体的服务或产品, 对方没有兑现承诺
(3) 目的是要求改动与处理, 不只是发泄

语气的标准是一句话
The general tone of a letter of complaint should be formal
and polite.

两条写作要求
(1) 把发生的事写细, 写实
Give a detailed and factual description of what had happened
to you.
(2) 用因果讲清为什么不满, 以及已经造成的后果
Employ cause and effect to tell the reasons (causes) why you
are upset and feel wronged, and emphasize the bad
consequences (effects) caused so far.

事实要能核对: 日期, 地点, 金额, 型号, 数量
因果要成对: 每个问题后面跟一句它造成的后果
熨斗信里这一对写得最短
As I need an iron on a daily basis, this faulty product
caused me great inconvenience.

感受也要落到具体的损失上
三篇范文的损失是 great inconvenience, a dangerous overdose,
soured my child's birthday party
都是能指认的损失, 不是空泛的愤怒

## 四段结构

表8-3 投诉信的四段

| 段 | 写什么 | 句式开头 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 第一段 | 目的, 不满, 概述 | I wish to express my dissatisfaction |
| 第二段 | 详细经过, 后果 | On using it for the first time |
| 第三段 | 解决办法, 期限 | I am writing to you to ask for |
| 第四段 | 附件 | Enclosed: |

一段说事, 二段说理, 三段要结果, 四段点单据

### 第一段 说明目的

第一段两句话: 为什么写, 对什么不满
不满要挂在具体的商品, 服务与日期上

I wish to express my dissatisfaction with/at the iron, which
I purchased from the Electrical Store in Newtown on 10
October, 2015.

I wish to express my dismay at the service at your
restaurant on Saturday, January 14.

I'm writing to lodge/file/put forward a complaint about what
I have experienced in your store on May 12.

I am writing to formally express my concern regarding an
incident that occurred on September 15 at ...School gate,
which I believe requires your immediate attention.

四个开头的骨架相同: 表态, 对象, 时间
lodge, file, put forward 三个动词任选其一, 意思一样
...School gate 是讲义里的省略写法, 校名留空

药房信换了说法, 点名到分店
I am writing to express my dismay at the service at your
Eden Hill branch on Saturday 14 January.

生日信第一段不表态, 只给事实, 不满留到第二段
两种开头都行, 但第一段读完, 读者要知道这封信投诉什么

### 第二段 详述经过

讲义给了三种写法, 按问题的多少挑一种

写法一 指出缺陷, 强调不便

On using the iron for the first time, I found that the
temperature control was faulty; it was not possible to set
it for any temperature apart from the highest, for cotton.

As I need an iron on a daily basis, this faulty product
caused me great inconvenience.

第一句说缺陷: 温度控制失灵, 除最高档外哪一档都设不了
第二句说后果: 每天都要用, 于是造成极大不便
分号把两个毛病并进一句, 比写成两句紧凑

写法二 先叙全过程, 再点后果

I often collect prescriptions from the pharmacy on behalf of
my grandmother, Mrs Elaine Bingham. On this occasion there
were two prescriptions: one for 10 x 50 mg Kendomol and one
for 50 x 100 mg Leoprone. I was served quickly even though
there appeared to be only one pharmacist on duty. However,
as I was leaving I saw that I had been given 500 mg tablets
of Kendomol. This is ten times stronger than the
prescription called for.

If I hadn't noticed the difference between the prescription
and the actual tablets, my grandmother could have taken a
dangerous overdose of Kendomol. I would be worried about
getting any future prescriptions at Eden Hill.

50 mg 与 500 mg 并排写, ten times 一算就清楚
后果用虚拟语气, 说没发生但可能发生的事, 比直接指责更重

写法三 逐条列事实与后果

Mr. Jones, we were very unhappy with the overall service.
Our party was scheduled to begin at 4:00; however, at 3:55,
the room was not ready. We had to wait outside with the
guests and parents for ten minutes. This created an
inconvenience for the parents, who had planned to drop their
children off.

In addition, the food was not served on time. While I can
understand a short delay, this soured my child's birthday
party. The worst is the fact that the staff was not friendly
at all, and seemed to be very rushed and annoyed. My guests
felt bad about such treatment.

三条问题: 房间没备好, 上菜不准时, 员工态度差
每条都是事实加后果: 等十分钟, 毁掉生日会, 客人难受
however, in addition, the worst is 把三条串起来, 轻重分明
问题多于两个时, 衔接词不是装饰

### 第三段 提出要求

第三段给两样东西: 要求与期限

As the iron clearly does not function as it should and
therefore does not comply with the legal standards of
product quality, I am writing to you to ask for a full
refund of the £35.99 retail price.

I look forward to hearing from you within the next two
weeks.

顺序是理由, 要求, 期限
退款金额写进句子里, 期限写两周

药房信不要钱, 要的是流程
I think it happened because there were not enough staff on
duty. I understand that mistakes happen but there needs to
be a minimum of two pharmacists at all times so all
prescriptions can be checked. I hope you can take steps to
make sure this mistake does not happen again.

先承认错误难免, 再提最低人手要求
抱怨里留一句理解, 语气稳, 要求反而更容易被接受

生日信要查清与保证, 再加一段礼貌的施压
I hope you would look into the matter, and make sure such an
incident will not be repeated.

I have not yet shared this on social media or any review
website. I wanted to provide you with an opportunity to
respond before I did so. You can reach me anytime at
(434)234-3324. Thank you.

意思是还没公开, 先给你回应的机会, 末尾留电话

### 第四段 点明附件

Enclosed: Copy of the receipt for iron

冒号后写具体内容, 附件不止一件时逐件列
正文里也可以先提一句: I have enclosed a receipt for your convenience.
信里提到的文件, 必须与实际寄出的一致

## 衔接词

表8-4 投诉信常用衔接词

| 类别 | 常用词 |
| --- | --- |
| 顺序 | First, second, third, finally |
| 顺序 | To begin with, then, next, finally |
| 递进 | also, besides, furthermore, moreover |
| 递进 | worse still, what's worst, in addition |
| 递进 | equally disappointing, another |
| 因果 | cause, lead to, result in |
| 因果 | is responsible for, is due to |
| 因果 | is a consequence of, affect, impact |
| 因果 | as a result, so, hence, consequently |
| 因果 | because of this, accordingly |
| 转折 | however, yet, even so, still |
| 转折 | nonetheless, while, on the contrary |
| 转折 | by contrast, beyond my expectation |
| 转折 | to my surprise |
| 时间 | next, afterward, finally, later, now |
| 时间 | when, a minute later, in the meantime |

顺序类讲义给了一个完整的例子
I loved England a lot. First I loved its museums. Secondly,
I loved its people. Finally I loved its tourist attractions.

例子不是投诉信, 只演示排法
投诉信里把 First, Second, Finally 放在每条问题的开头

递进类专管层层加码
worse still 与 what's worst 引出最糟的一条
equally disappointing 说明另一条同样让人失望

因果类用得最多, 原因在前, 结果在后
前面例8-1 第三段的 as 从句就是标准写法
cause, lead to, result in 写某件事导致什么
is due to, is a consequence of 写某个结果由什么造成

转折类用来让步
While I can understand a short delay, this soured my child's
birthday party.
先承认可以理解, 再指出仍然不能接受, 这是最实用的句式

时间类多用在叙事式的中段
at 4:00, at 3:55, for ten minutes 把经过按钟点排开

## 范文

三篇都出自讲义, 英文逐字照抄, 只为版面按 60 字符折行

例8-1: 熨斗的质量投诉与退款

```letter

Dear Sir or Madam:

I wish to express my dissatisfaction with the iron, which I
purchased from the Electrical Store in Newtown on 10
October, 2015.

On using it for the first time, I found that the temperature
control was faulty; it was not possible to set it for any
temperature apart from the highest, for cotton.

When I attempted to return the iron to the store, the
cashier said that the store was unable to replace it or
offer me a refund but advised me that it could be sent away
for repair. As I need an iron on a daily basis, and it was
not clear how long a repair would take, this option was
unsatisfactory. As the iron clearly does not function as it
should and therefore does not comply with the legal
standards of product quality, I am writing to you to ask for
a full refund of the £35.99 retail price.

I look forward to hearing from you within the next two
weeks.

Yours truly,
Jenny Hawkins
Enclosed: Copy of the receipt for iron
```

四段齐全, 一段一件事
第三段把退货受阻也写进去, 商家的处理本身就是一条理由
退款的金额与期限都写实, 敬语与署名靠左

例8-2: 生日会的服务投诉

讲义只印了正文, 称呼与结尾从略

```letter
We recently celebrated our son's 6th birthday party at The
Burgeroo at 32451 Jefferson Road. Our party was scheduled
for Saturday, May 17th, at 4:00 PM. I have enclosed a
receipt for your convenience.

Mr. Jones, we were very unhappy with the overall service.
Our party was scheduled to begin at 4:00; however, at 3:55,
the room was not ready. We had to wait outside with the
guests and parents for ten minutes. This created an
inconvenience for the parents, who had planned to drop their
children off.

In addition, the food was not served on time. While I can
understand a short delay, this soured my child's birthday
party. The staff was not friendly about it, and seemed to be
very rushed and annoyed.

In the past, we have enjoyed eating at The Burgeroo. The
service is usually quick and friendly. Up until this
negative experience, it was my son's favorite restaurant. I
hope you would look into the matter, and make sure such an
incident will not be repeated.

I have not yet shared this on social media or any review
website. I wanted to provide you with an opportunity to
respond before I did so. You can reach me anytime at
(434)234-3324. Thank you.
```

第一段给时间, 地点与单据, 一句一件事
第二段先总说一句 unhappy, 再按 however, in addition 铺开
第三段先提过去的好印象, 给店家台阶, 再提要求
第四段说明还没发到网上, 留电话, 给对方最后一次机会
写法三的 the worst is 一句在范文里降了调门
改成 The staff was not friendly about it, 只陈述不评价

例8-3: 药房发错剂量

```letter

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to express my dismay at the service at your
Eden Hill branch on Saturday 14 January.

I often collect prescriptions from the pharmacy on behalf of
my grandmother, Mrs Elaine Bingham. On this occasion there
were two prescriptions: one for 10 x 50 mg Kendomol and one
for 50 x 100 mg Leoprone. I was served quickly even though
there appeared to be only one pharmacist on duty. However,
as I was leaving I saw that I had been given 500 mg tablets
of Kendomol. This is ten times stronger than the
prescription called for.

If I hadn't noticed the difference between the prescription
and the actual tablets, my grandmother could have taken a
dangerous overdose of Kendomol. I would be worried about
getting any future prescriptions at Eden Hill.

The pharmacist apologised and corrected the mistake but I
wanted to bring it to your attention. I think it happened
because there were not enough staff on duty. I understand
that mistakes happen but there needs to be a minimum of two
pharmacists at all times so all prescriptions can be
checked.

I hope you can take steps to make sure this mistake does not
happen again.

Yours faithfully,
Roger Bingham
```

正文五段, 四件事都在: 目的, 经过与后果, 要求, 收尾
第二段按时间走: 常做的事, 这次的处方, 拿到的药
第四段先给原因, 再提最低人手要求
称呼用逗号, 敬语用 Yours faithfully, 两者配套

三篇的敬语不同, 与各自的称呼配套
一封里只取一套, 不混用

## 写作步骤与检查

动笔前先把事实列成清单
日期, 地点, 人名, 金额, 型号, 数量, 单据
事实不齐, 第二段就写不具体

写的时候按这个顺序

(1) 用一句话定目的: 投诉什么, 要求什么
(2) 选一种中段写法: 缺陷式, 叙事式, 逐条式
(3) 一条问题配一条后果, 成对写
(4) 三条以上用衔接词排好顺序
(5) 第三段写具体要求与期限
(6) 附件在正文提一句, 信末列一行

交稿前逐条检查

(1) 第一段两句内说清写信目的
(2) 事实带时间, 地点, 数量或金额
(3) 每个问题后面有后果
(4) 要求具体: 退款, 更换, 道歉或改进
(5) 期限写明, 通常两周
(6) 附件与正文提到的文件一致
(7) 语气正式有礼, 没有辱骂与威胁
(8) 称呼, 敬语, 署名与版式统一

## 练习

练习一 行李丢失

You travelled by plane last week and your suitcase was lost.
You have still heard nothing from the airline company. Write
to the airline in 180 words and explain what happened.
Describe your suitcase and tell them what was in it. Tell
them what you expect them to do for you.
